FAQ 
Who is Go Beyond for? + 
Go Beyond is designed primarily for Store Managers, Assistant Store Managers and experienced retail leaders who are thinking about what comes next in their careers. 
Do I need corporate experience? + 
No. Go Beyond was created to help retail leaders understand opportunities beyond the store and recognize how their existing experience may connect to them. 
It also covers what new skills to develop to scale your career. 
What is included in a $150 path? + 
Every focused path includes Foundation of Success, your chosen specialization and Tools for a Strong Job Search. 
You get a strong foundational knowledge, plus practical tools to help you apply for jobs efficiently. 
What is included in the $350 Full Program? + 
All five modules: Foundation of Success, Marketing, Retail Operations, Field Leadership and Tools for a Strong Job Search. 
Is Go Beyond self-paced? + 
Yes. You can work through the course on your own schedule within 9 months. 
How long do I have access? + 
You receive 9 months of access. 
Are there knowledge checks? + 
Yes. Short self-checks are included after modules/chapters to help reinforce what you learned. 
What if I don’t know which path is right for me? + 
Choose the Full Go Beyond Program to explore all three career directions, or book a free 15-minute Path Clarity Call with us. 
Does completing Go Beyond guarantee a corporate or field role? + 
No. Go Beyond provides education, practical frameworks and career-positioning tools, but individual hiring outcomes depend on many factors.
